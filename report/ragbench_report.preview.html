RAG Search-Flow Comparison

Chunking and embedding are fixed; only the search flow changes. Up to 8 flows are compared on answer quality and cost across three RAGBench datasets and a reduced BrowseComp-Plus.

Key findings

Numbers come from the charts and paired comparisons below. Differences are always "first minus second", with 95% confidence intervals in parentheses. Each RAGBench dataset has only 66 questions, so only large differences are visible.

Things to know when reading this

    How the metrics are computed
    faithfulness
    The judge splits the answer into atomic claims and checks each against the evidence the group actually used; the score is the supported share. Answers with no claims (e.g. "insufficient evidence") are excluded from the mean and reported separately as the abstain rate.
    fully faithful rate
    Share of questions where every claim in the answer is supported by the evidence.
    completeness
    Of the RAGBench-annotated relevant sentences, how many the answer conveys. The reference sentences are independent of retrieval, so a flow that fails to retrieve the key information scores low.
    mean rounds
    Retrieval rounds executed at stop time; for ReAct, the number of searches.
    truncated by R_max
    Share of questions that ran to the round limit (6) before stopping.
    cost per 1k questions
    GLM, Jev and query-embedding cost per 1,000 questions, computed as tokens times list price. The GLM output price already includes reasoning tokens (counted once).
    G1–G8
    G1 single round; G2 ReAct; G3 multi-round with an LLM stop judge; G4 multi-round with a Jev stop judge; G5 Jev stop judge plus Jev per-chunk retrieval; G6 fixed number of rounds; G7 stop judged against gold (upper-bound reference); G8 embedding top-200 shortlist re-ranked by Jev (BrowseComp-Plus only).
